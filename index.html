Welcome to Waveshore

A little closer to nature. A lot closer to peace.

At Waveshore Beach Resort, we believe a perfect getaway brings together the warmth of home and the magic of the sea. Just steps from a beautiful beach, our stay is a refreshing escape for families, couples and adventure lovers.

Settle into a clean, cozy room, enjoy fresh and delicious meals with local flavours, or choose a more outdoorsy escape with tent camping. Come for the sea, stay for the feeling.

How can I enquire or book a stay?

Use the Enquire Now or WhatsApp buttons to contact the resort team and discuss your preferred dates and stay requirements.

What kind of stay do you offer?

Waveshore Beach Resort offers a homestay with clean, cozy rooms, an in-house kitchen and tent camping for guests who prefer an outdoor stay.

Are meals available?

The resort website describes an in-house kitchen serving fresh meals with local flavours. Contact the team for current menu and meal arrangements.

Yes, contact the resort directly to discuss your group size, dates and available arrangements.
